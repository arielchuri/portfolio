Sculptural Glass & Optical Dispersion

Real-time double-refraction crystal studio built with Three.js and custom GLSL shaders. Front-to-back internal light transport renders across a 16-band Interleaved Gradient Noise spectrum inside a 360° procedural color field.

  • 15 Glass Forms: Curved Ribbon, Star Fruit, Dragon Fruit, Torus, Hour Glass, Cylinder, Helices, and faceted crystals.
  • Optical Lens Controls: Camera focal length, zoom, 12-band radial lens chromatic aberration, and internal thickness.
  • Studio Mode & Export: Light/black studio switching, viscous cursor-movement drag, and full-resolution PNG capture.

Canvas / Local-First Web App

Dank Elf VTT

A zero-build RPG virtual tabletop built with vanilla HTML, CSS, and Canvas—designed around physical paper sheets, mimeo-violet drop shadows, and fast tabletop improvisation.

  • Dynamic Sheets & Maps: Editable cream character sheets, goldenrod notes, and cyan-blue vector map drafting with tokens and grid/hex overlays.
  • 3D Polyhedral Dice: Tabletop roller supporting d2–d50, d%, and DCC Zocchi solids with P2P sync.
  • Local-First & P2P: Persists locally via localStorage/IndexedDB with JSON import/export and WebRTC multiplayer support.
