## General Observations

### Tone & Professionalism
Overall, your portfolio maintains a professional and confident tone. The writing effectively communicates your expertise and accomplishments.

**Strengths:**
- Clear, concise descriptions of projects
- Good use of industry terminology
- Professional without being overly formal
- Strong action verbs ("led," "created," "designed," "implemented")

**Suggestions:**
- Consider being more specific about outcomes/metrics where possible (e.g., "improved user satisfaction by X%" or "reduced bounce rate by Y%")
- Some sections could benefit from more concrete examples of impact

### Consistency Notes
- You alternate between "UX/UI" and "UX" - consider standardizing
- Some project descriptions use first person ("I led...") while others use passive voice - maintaining consistency could strengthen the narrative
